Fifty-three years of shaping minds, building futures, and inspiring generations! As Mindanao State University - General Santos celebrates its 53rd Founding Anniversary, the Junior Information Technology Society joins the university community in honoring its rich history, enduring legacy, and continued pursuit of excellence.

As we look back on the milestones that have shaped our institution, we also look forward to a future driven by innovation, collaboration, as well as the limitless possibilities of technology. May we continue to uphold the values of learning, service, and progress as we help shape the next chapter of our university's story.

Here's to 53 years of growth and countless more years of making a difference! 💙

Happy 53rd Founding Anniversary, MSU–GenSan! 🎂🎓
