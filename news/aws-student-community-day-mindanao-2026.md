The Junior Information Technology Society (JITS), in partnership with AWS Student Builder Group – UP Mindanao, is inviting interested students to participate in AWS Student Community Day Mindanao 2026.

The event will be held on November 28, 2026, at Ateneo de Davao University in Davao City. JITS will cover registration and transportation expenses for selected participants.

Submitting the form does not guarantee selection. Two students from each year level of the BS Information Technology program and one student from the BS Physics program will be selected at random.

Interested students who are willing to participate should complete the form and ensure all information is accurate and complete.
